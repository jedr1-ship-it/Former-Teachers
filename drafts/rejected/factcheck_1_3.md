## MUST-FIX

### MF-1. Aurora null verdict attributed to the trial itself instead of the WWC re-review
- **Draft (line 19, Key take-aways):** "while the one randomised trial of retirees as new-teacher mentors found no significant effects (DeCesare, McClelland and Randel, 2017)."
- **Research base:** The REL trial itself reported a **statistically significant year-1 mathematics effect** ("nearly 12 percent of the academic growth in math… equivalent to one month of additional instruction"); the "no statistically significant positive findings" verdict in reading, mathematics and teacher retention is the **What Works Clearinghouse re-review (August 2019, Study #88756, Standards 4.0)**, and the base explicitly requires the pairing: "a qualified null under WWC standards … (DeCesare, McClelland and Randel, 2017; WWC, 2019, Study #88756)".
- **Evidence:** lit_retiree_mentoring.md lines 26–32 (esp. line 31); ch5_synthesis.md line 35 (Lesson 9) and lines 96–98 (D7: domains and the misdating warning).
- **Fix:** either add the qualifier and citation — "found no significant effects under What Works Clearinghouse standards (DeCesare, McClelland and Randel, 2017; WWC, 2019)" — or reword to "was a qualified null". As written, a finding is attributed to a source that reports the opposite for year-1 maths.
- **Severity: must-fix** (misattributed finding).

### MF-2. Experience Corps volunteers described as "many of them former educators"
- **Draft (line 177, §3.3):** "Experience Corps, which places trained volunteers aged 50 and over, many of them former educators…" (repeated implicitly by placing the programme under a retiree-teacher argument).
- **Research base:** the dedicated case verification states former-teacher status is **not an eligibility requirement** and that the "former teachers volunteers" framing "overstates; volunteers are older adults, *some* of whom are retired educators (share [UNVERIFIED])". Note an internal conflict in the base: lit_retiree_mentoring.md line 46 says "(many former educators)", but cases_mentoring.md is the file that checked this specific claim and flags "many" as unverifiable.
- **Evidence:** cases_mentoring.md line 103; lit_retiree_mentoring.md line 46.
- **Fix:** "some of them former educators" (or "including retired educators").
- **Severity: must-fix** (share is unverified; "many" exceeds the evidence and props up the chapter's retiree framing).

### MF-3. USD 278/month stipend misattributed to Morrow-Howell et al. (2009) and generalised
- **Draft (line 177, §3.3):** "works through fixed weekly sessions and modest stipends of up to about USD 278 per month (Morrow-Howell et al., 2009)."
- **Research base:** the USD 278/month figure is pinned to **WWC (2010)** and described as historical AmeriCorps/CNCS **stipended slots at some sites** ("some sites use AmeriCorps/CNCS funding streams (historically, stipended slots up to USD 278/month) (WWC, 2010)"), not as a general programme design feature, and not to Morrow-Howell et al. lit_return_incentives.md line 64 carries the number without a pinpoint citation.
- **Evidence:** cases_mentoring.md line 100; lit_return_incentives.md line 64.
- **Fix:** re-source to WWC (2010) and hedge: "modest stipends — historically up to about USD 278 per month at stipended sites (WWC, 2010)". The number itself is fine.
- **Severity: must-fix** (wrong source attached to a printed number).

### C-4. NSW "placed 145 returners across more than 600 schools"
- **Draft (line 18, Key take-aways):** "placed 145 returners across more than 600 schools (NSW Government, 2024)."
- **Research base:** official phrasing is that the 145 perform casual and temporary work "in more than 600 schools across the state" — the pool collectively covers 600+ schools; the case file instructs "phrase as coverage, not one-to-one placement". The draft's "across" arguably does this, but "placed … across" can be read as placements into 600 schools. Also the case file labels the source NSW Government (2024a).
- **Evidence:** cases_nsw_bahamas.md lines 28–34, 45.
- **Fix (optional):** "placed 145 returners in casual and temporary roles covering more than 600 schools". Cosmetic.

### C-5. North Carolina "around 3 500 retirees" — internal conflict in the research base (draft follows the superseding file; no draft change required)
- **Draft (line 15, Key take-aways):** "brought back around 3 500 retirees" (Jarrold-Grapes and Mahler, 2024).
- **Research base:** ch5_synthesis.md line 25 (a superseding fix file) says "~3,500 returners, just over 35% of the retirement-eligible returning by 2009"; but lit_reserve.md line 55 records "the 2,694 returnees (81% female, disproportionately Black, 31 years' average experience)" from the same article. The two counts may refer to different samples (all returners vs the analysed full-time returner cohort), but the base never reconciles them. The draft correctly follows the fix file; flagging so the team reconciles 3,500 vs 2,694 against the article before publication — Box 3.1 uses the 2,694-cohort descriptors (31 years, 81% women) without the count, so the box is internally safe.
- **Severity: cosmetic/watch** (no draft edit mandated by the supersession rule).
