## C. Register breaks vs style_oecd.md (7)

**11. Line 377 (Recommendation 3).** "The one country that has run both margins side by side priced them for everyone else." Journalistic; "priced them for everyone else" is trader's argot, not OECD register.
> Rewrite: "The one country that has run both margins side by side, Portugal, provides the clearest available comparison of their yields."

**12. Line 385 (Recommendation 7).** "a general advertising campaign is what governments buy when they have not looked." Editorial swipe; OECD recommendations are conditions and trade-offs, never barbs.
> Rewrite: "The employer's payroll and registration records are the best returner database most systems will ever own; general advertising campaigns reach the same people later, at higher cost and with more deadweight."

**13. Line 379 (Recommendation 4).** Two aphorisms in one paragraph: "Each screen added is take-up subtracted." and "Bonuses layered on top of unreformed earnings rules pay people to fight the rules." One is voice; two is performance.
> Fix: keep the first, rewrite the second: "Bonuses layered on top of unreformed earnings rules work against the very rules that deter return, and are better sequenced after them."

**14. Line 281.** "The 63-versus-2 200 asymmetry permits a precise conclusion." Overclaims: a single-country, non-causal comparison does not permit precision; this breaks house rule 2 (hedge like a researcher) in the opposite direction from "it depends".
> Rewrite (if the sentence survives hit 3's cut): "The 63-versus-2 200 asymmetry points one way: at comparable money, preventing an exit is far easier than reversing one." Better, apply hit 3 and delete.

**15. Line 253.** "since 2022 they have been courted hardest in Europe" — "courted" is tabloid shorthand.
> Rewrite: "and since 2022 European systems have pursued them most actively; in one country the yields are now audited."

**16. Line 355.** "The directive created no new entitlement, which is the point, since much of what deters returners is process…" — "which is the point" is conversational.
> Rewrite: "The directive created no new entitlement, and that is its significance: much of what deters returners is process, and process can be changed by instruction rather than statute." (Or, colon-free: "…and therein lies its significance. Much of what deters returners is process, and process can be changed by instruction rather than statute.")

**17. Line 15 (take-away 5).** "For retirees, pension rules largely are the policy." The adverb placement is contorted; the same formula recurs at line 303.
> Rewrite: "For retirees, the pension re-employment rules are most of the policy." Also, line 135 "robust" ("the causal literature… is robust") is a stock AI adjective — use "well identified" or "solid"; line 139 "strikingly similar terms" → "similar terms"; line 97 "the single most useful fact in the destinations literature" → "the most useful fact in the destinations literature".
