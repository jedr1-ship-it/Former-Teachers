**Table 3.2. The four regulatory dials that price a return, by system**

| System (instrument) | Pay: step and pension concurrency | Exclusion: waits, thresholds, screens | Intensity: caps on quantity | Licence: lapse, refresh, waiver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Austria (crediting rules; Länder contracts) | Ex-civil-servants: full pension + starting salary (pension-generating service excluded from crediting); ex-contract teachers: service credited, broadly pre-retirement pay | None formal | Reduced hours common in new fixed-term contracts | — |
| Portugal (Decreto-Lei 51/2024, Art. 5) | Pension kept + pay indexed to first career step, pro-rated by hours | Retired five years or less; annual quota | Pro-rating by teaching hours | — |
| North Carolina, US (RTW window, 1999-2009) | Full salary + full pension during the window; half-salary part-time earnings cap before and after | First year only: low-performing schools, certification match | None during the window | — |
| Georgia, US (HB 385 / SB 150) | Full pension + placement on the salary schedule | 30 years' service; one-year break; three highest-need subjects per region (district-determined from 2026); sunset 2030 | Full-time only | Certification required |
| New Mexico, US (HB 73 / SB 133) | Pension kept; no salary limits on the newest route | 90-day layout; no pre-arranged return; board approval | Routes at ≤0.25 FTE, under USD 25 000, or no limits for up to 60 months | — |
| Michigan, US (PA 184 / PA 147) | Pension and insurance subsidy kept; no earnings limit after the wait | Bona fide termination; six-month wait (reverts to nine months in 2028 unless renewed); USD 15 100 earnings cap during the wait | None after the wait | — |
| Louisiana, US (Acts 394/2024, 344/2026) | Pension + salary in certified critical-shortage posts | 12-month wait; fewer-than-three-certified-applicants test; no-impact route at 65+ from 2026 | Earnings cap of 50% of final average compensation outside shortage routes | General certification suffices from 2026 |
| Virginia, US (Va. Code § 51.1-155; ED-3) | Pension + salary in critical-shortage positions (statutory since 2001) | Six-month break (previously twelve); no pre-arranged return; closed to early-retirement-incentive retirees; sunset 2028 | Full-time critical-shortage posts | Lapsed licences fast-tracked under the 2022 directive |
| Ontario, Canada (Teachers' Pension Act, Sch. 1) | Pension kept within the cap | — | 50 days per school year (95 in 1990-2012 for the first three years, and 2020/21-2022/23); movable only by co-sponsor agreement | — |
| France (cumul emploi-retraite) | Capped pension-earnings combination unless full-rate conditions are met; new pension rights accrue since 2023 | Six months before re-employment with the last employer | — | — |
| England, UK (Teachers' Pension Scheme) | Abatement of final-salary Age/Premature benefits above the salary of reference; career-average benefits never abated | — | Earnings limit rather than day cap | — |
| New Zealand (Education and Training Act 2020; Teaching Council policy) | — | — | 20 half-days per year of relief teaching without a practising certificate; extension possible with a job offer | Refresh required mainly without a job offer; certificate fees paid by government to mid-2028 |
| Ireland (career break scheme; annual information notes) | Substitute pay at regular rates; increments accrue under general rules | Career-break status | Substitute-work limits suspended annually since at least 2021/22 | — |

Note: "—" indicates no provision specific to returners identified in the sources consulted. The table records rule design, not effect; published take-up under each rule appears in Table 3.3 where it exists.
