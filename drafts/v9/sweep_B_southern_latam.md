# Country sweep, group B: Southern Europe, Türkiye, Israel, Latin America

Systematic sweep for multi-year evidence on the evolution of the teacher leaving rate (exits from the teaching profession or nearest equivalent). All sources accessed 28 August 2026. Secondary or abstract-only items are marked as such. A cross-country anchor used below: OECD, [How severe are teacher shortages across countries? (Education at a Glance 2025)](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/education-at-a-glance-2025_1c0d9c79-en/full-report/how-severe-are-teacher-shortages-across-countries_781f4a97.html) reports that across 19 systems an average 6.5% of fully qualified teachers left the profession in 2022/23, with France, Greece, Ireland and Israel under 3% (single reference year, not a trend series; summarised in the [NEU press note](https://neu.org.uk/latest/press-releases/education-glance-2025-oecd-data-confirms-teacher-retention-crisis), secondary).

## Spain: NO multi-year evidence found

1. **What exists.** Nothing official and no register-based academic series. Tahull Fort, J. and Molina-Luque, F. (2025), "El abandono de la docencia y la escasez de profesores", *Antropología Experimental* 25, 55-68, [article page](https://revistaselectronicas.ujaen.es/index.php/rae/article/view/9423), states explicitly that "En España, no se publican datos oficiales sobre esta temática" (no official data are published on this topic) and that decentralisation to the autonomous communities compounds the gap; its own evidence is qualitative (three interviews) plus union material from Catalonia.
2. **Span.** None.
3. **Level and range.** None. Spain does not appear among the 19 systems reporting attrition in Education at a Glance 2025.
4. **Trend verdict.** Not assessable; no series exists.
5. **Definition.** n/a.
6. **Searches before concluding absence.** Spanish: "abandono de la profesión docente España tasa estadística evolución profesorado"; "excedencias y renuncias profesorado" (embedded in the above); ""abandono de la profesión" OR "attrition" docentes España/México estudio bajas maestros renuncias". English: "teacher attrition Spain longitudinal study leave the profession rate data lack official statistics". Checked: Ministerio de Educación press and statistics results (all hits concern pupil early school leaving, "abandono educativo temprano"), Fundación SM survey work (intentions only, not exits), UNESCO/STECyL coverage (global figures only). Conclusion: absence of any multi-year leaving-rate evidence, corroborated by the peer-reviewed statement above.

## Portugal: no leaving-rate series; retirement-exit counts and projections only

1. **What exists.**
   - Polígrafo fact-check (2022) of the Fenprof claim that "more than 10,000 teachers abandoned the profession in 10 years": rated Verdadeiro, but the checkers stress the figure mixes resignations and retirements. [Fact-check](https://poligrafo.sapo.pt/fact-check/mais-de-10-mil-professores-abandonaram-a-profissao-em-10-anos) (secondary).
   - DGEEC and Nova SBE, *Estudo de diagnóstico de necessidades docentes de 2021 a 2030* (November 2021), [PDF mirror](https://spzn.pt/uploads/documentos/documento_1637162470_8679.pdf); and the follow-up *Estudo de diagnóstico de necessidades docentes de 2025 a 2034* (2025), [DGEEC PDF](https://www.dgeec.medu.pt/api/ficheiros/68e4f43609ef6c29d2205bc0). Verified from the 2021 full text: the model treats exits as retirement only (all teachers assumed to leave at the statutory age, plus an estimated early-retirement rate); it does not publish an observed non-retirement leaving-rate series. Of circa 120,000 teachers in 2018/19, about 40% were projected to retire by 2030/31; the 2025 study projects a need for about 38,000 new teachers by 2034/35.
   - Annual retirement counts (DGEEC data via press): almost 3,400 retirements in 2022/23, [Diário de Notícias](https://www.dn.pt/sociedade/aposentaram-se-quase-3400-professores-no-ano-letivo-passado--16703660.html) (secondary); about 3,974 in 2024 and over 3,600 in 2025, the two heaviest years of the decade, [Diário de Notícias, 2026](https://www.dn.pt/sociedade/reformas-na-educacao-a-caminho-de-recorde-ate-agosto-serao-2300-professores-a-deixar-a-profissao) (secondary).
2. **Span.** Retirement counts circa 2013-2025; projections 2021-2034.
3. **Level and range.** Retirements roughly 2,000 to 4,000 a year on a teaching stock of about 120,000-130,000 (roughly 1.5-3% a year); no figure isolates pre-retirement profession exits.
4. **Trend verdict.** Rising exits, but retirement-driven (workforce over 55% aged 50+). Not assessable as a leaving rate proper.
5. **Definition.** Sector exits dominated by retirement; the 10,000-in-10-years figure conflates resignations with retirements. Not a proper leaving rate.
6. **Additional searches.** "abandono da profissão docente DGEEC estatísticas saídas professores"; "professores saíram do sistema educativo número anual DGEEC aposentações rescisões"; "teacher attrition Portugal leaving the profession rate DGEEC OR EDULOG OR Nova SBE". DGEEC's Perfil do Docente and Educação em Números series contain stocks, ages and entries, not exits by cause.

## Italy: NO multi-year leaving-rate evidence found

1. **What exists.** Only administrative counts of cessazioni dal servizio (service terminations), which are dominated by retirements and published operationally, not as a statistical series: e.g. the annual ministry circulars and previsional data on cessazioni from 1 September of each year, [MIM USR Marche notice for 2026](https://www.mim.gov.it/web/miur-usr-marche/-/cessazioni-dal-servizio-dal-1-settembre-2026-i-dati-previsionali-relativi-al-personale-docente-educativo-amministrativo-tecnico-e-ausiliario). A press item reproduced by FLC CGIL ([ItaliaOggi, "Riprende quota la fuga dalla scuola"](https://m.flcgil.it/rassegna-stampa/nazionale/italiaoggi-riprende-quota-la-fuga-dalla-scuola.flc), page inaccessible at access date, secondary) reports dimissioni volontarie rising from 13,954 to 19,785 in one year (about +27%), but in the Italian system "dimissioni volontarie" are overwhelmingly resignations filed to draw a pension, and the reference years could not be verified. Tuttoscuola commentary ([La grande fuga degli insegnanti](https://www.tuttoscuola.com/insegnanti-grande-fuga/), secondary) contains no Italian series. Italy is not among the systems reporting attrition in Education at a Glance 2025.
2. **Span.** None as a leaving-rate series.
3. **Level and range.** Cessazioni of the order of 20,000-30,000 a year on about 850,000 teachers (2-3%), nearly all retirement.
4. **Trend verdict.** Not assessable; no profession-exit series.
5. **Definition.** Administrative terminations (retirements plus pension-motivated resignations); not a proper leaving rate.
6. **Searches before concluding absence.** Italian: "dimissioni volontarie insegnanti statistiche tasso abbandono professione docente"; "docenti dimissioni volontarie cessazioni dati Ministero Istruzione anni"; ""dimissioni volontarie" docenti di ruolo numero anno orizzontescuola dati 2015 2022"; "tuttoscuola fuga dalla scuola docenti dimissioni dati annuali serie storica". Downloaded and read the MIM Nota cessazioni a.s. 2023/24 (administrative circular only, no statistics). Checked MIUR/INVALSI leads: INVALSI publishes no teacher-exit data. Conclusion: absence of multi-year leaving-rate evidence; only retirement-dominated cessazioni counts.

## Greece: multi-year resignation counts exist, but they are retirement applications

1. **What exists.** Annual counts of teacher resignation applications (αιτήσεις παραίτησης), compiled from ministry data by education analysts and published by Alfavita: [2024 article with tables](https://www.alfavita.gr/ekpaideysi/442545_paraitiseis-ekpaideytikon-perissoteres-apo-persi-oi-fetines-paraitiseis) and [2026 article with tables](https://www.alfavita.gr/ekpaideysi/506997_ekpaideytikoi-perissoteres-apo-persi-oi-fetines-paraitiseis-ekpaideytikon-pinakes) (both secondary, compiled from official announcements). Figures: 2023: 1,684 primary plus 2,083 secondary (3,767); 2024: 1,801 plus 2,244 (about 4,045; full-year total reported as 4,549); 2025: 4,716; 2026: 5,492 (record; primary 2,679, secondary 2,813).
2. **Span.** Circa 2023-2026 in the retrieved tables (the outlets track the series annually).
3. **Level and range.** Roughly 3,800 to 5,500 a year on a stock of about 150,000-165,000 state teachers, that is roughly 2.5-3.5% a year. Education at a Glance 2025 puts Greek attrition under 3% of fully qualified teachers in 2022/23.
4. **Trend verdict.** Rising counts: from about 3,800 (2023) to 5,492 (2026), about +45% in three years, described in the Greek press as a historic peak.
5. **Definition.** Not a proper leaving rate. In the Greek civil service a teacher "resigns" (παραίτηση) in order to draw a pension, so these are overwhelmingly retirement exits (παραιτήσεις-συνταξιοδοτήσεις); genuinely early, non-pension exits are not separated in any source found.
6. **Additional searches.** "παραιτήσεις εκπαιδευτικών Ελλάδα στατιστικά αριθμός"; esos.gr and especial.gr tag pages (especial.gr blocked at access date). No official Hellenic statistical series on profession exits found.

## France: verified and extended; strong official series

1. **What exists.**
   - **Primary.** DEPP, *Panorama statistique des personnels de l'enseignement scolaire 2021-2022* (October 2022), chapter 11 "Les départs volontaires", Table 11.1, [publication page](https://www.education.gouv.fr/depp/panorama-statistique-des-personnels-de-l-enseignement-scolaire-2021-2022-343054), [PDF mirror](https://se-unsa67.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/panorama-statistique-des-personnels-de-l-enseignement-scolaire-2021-2022-118135.pdf). Full verified series of voluntary definitive departures (démissions plus, from 2019, ruptures conventionnelles) of public-sector teachers: 2008-09: 364 (0.05%); 2009-10: 436 (0.06%); 2010-11: 560 (0.08%); 2011-12: 504 (0.07%); 2012-13: 399 (0.06%); 2013-14: 523 (0.08%); 2014-15: 804 (0.12%); 2015-16: 1,002 (0.14%); 2016-17: 1,232 (0.18%); 2017-18: 1,417 (0.20%); 2018-19: 1,664 (0.23%); 2019-20: 1,598 (0.23%); 2020-21: 2,411 (0.34%, of which 1,584 démissions and 827 ruptures conventionnelles; primary 1,499, 0.43%; secondary 912, 0.26%; stagiaires 749, 3.44%).
   - Sénat, avis budgétaire PLF 2023, [Enseignement scolaire](https://www.senat.fr/rap/a22-120-31/a22-120-318.html), reproduces the DEPP series (364 in 2008-09 to 2,411 in 2020-21) and the rupture conventionnelle breakdown.
   - Sénat, rapport PLF 2026, [Enseignement scolaire](https://www.senat.fr/rap/l25-139-313/l25-139-3138.html), citing DEPP: 2,859 resignations in 2023-24, about 0.3% of teachers; stagiaire resignation rate 4% in 2023-24 against 2.6% in 2017 and 0.9% in 2008.
   - Secondary corroboration of circa 0.40% in 2022: [MFP note](https://www.mfp.fr/presse/evolution-inquietante-des-demissions-denseignants/) and [professeurs-des-ecoles.com](https://www.professeurs-des-ecoles.com/2023/11/28/demissions-des-enseignants-hausse-de-567-en-10-ans-une-tendance-alarmante/) (both secondary, citing DEPP; the 2022 breakdown given is 0.11 points stagiaires, 0.08 titulaires under 5 years, 0.21 titulaires over 5 years).
   - **Correction to our holdings.** The series is published in the DEPP *Panorama statistique* / *Bilan social* volumes, not in a numbered Note d'information; no DEPP Note d'information dedicated to démissions was found, so the holding "DEPP Note d'information numbers" should be re-labelled accordingly.
   - **Fuller exit measure.** Same Panorama, chapter 10: retirements in 2021-22 were about 7,900 in the public first degree (2.2% of agents) and about 8,000 in the second degree (2.3%), with private-sector rates of 2.7% and 2.9%. Total definitive exits (retirement plus voluntary) are therefore roughly 2.5-2.7% a year, and Education at a Glance 2025 reports total French attrition under 3% of fully qualified teachers in 2022/23.
2. **Span.** 2008-09 to 2023-24, annual.
3. **Level and range.** Voluntary exits 0.05% to about 0.34-0.40%; total exits including retirement about 2.5%.
4. **Trend verdict.** Rising sharply from a very low base: voluntary departures up roughly sevenfold in rate terms (0.05% to about 0.34-0.40%) between 2008-09 and 2022-24, with stagiaire resignations up from 0.9% to 4%; total exits remain low and retirement-dominated. Our held range "~0.06% to ~0.4%, 2008-2023" is confirmed within rounding (the exact 2008-09 figure is 0.05%).
5. **Definition.** Voluntary definitive departures from the Éducation nationale by tenured and trainee civil-servant teachers (resignations plus negotiated terminations): a sector-exit measure close to a profession-leaving rate for the public system, excluding retirements and moves to private schools.

## Türkiye: multi-year counts of leavers exist; no official rate series

1. **What exists.**
   - MEB 2023 Faaliyet Raporu (annual activity report) figures, reported by Ahmet Ünlü in [Yeni Şafak](https://www.yenisafak.com/yazarlar/ahmet-unlu/mebin-2023-yili-faaliyet-raporunda-ogretmenlerle-ilgili-neler-yer-aliyor-4607927) and education outlets ([Öğretmenler Sitesi](https://ogretmenlersitesi.com/haber/meb-son-10-yilda-sistemden-ayrilan-emekli-olan-ogretmen-sayisi-belli-oldu-345896), secondary): teachers leaving MEB by all causes (retirement, resignation, dismissal, transfer, death): 2012: 8,800; 2013: 7,877; 2014: 7,975; 2015: 10,988; 2016: 44,144 (mass dismissals after the coup attempt); 2017: 19,217; 2018: 8,982; 2019: 15,019; 2020: 15,021; 2021: 15,063; 2022: 5,771; 2023: 23,670 (EYT early-retirement window and the 3600 ek gösterge reform); 2024/25: 10,889.
   - Resignations only (istifa), parliamentary answer reported by [Elips Haber](https://www.elipshaber.com/meb-acikladi-son-uc-yilda-kac-ogretmen-istifa-etti) (secondary): of 37,000 teachers leaving MEB in 2022-2024, 33,880 retired, 2,671 resigned and 867 transferred between institutions, that is under 1,000 resignations a year on a stock above one million (about 0.1% a year).
   - OECD, [Education at a Glance 2024](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/09/education-at-a-glance-2024_5ea68448/c00cad36-en.pdf): Türkiye reported the lowest attrition of participating countries, about 2% of teachers leaving the education system in 2022/23 (single year).
2. **Span.** 2012-2025, annual counts.
3. **Level and range.** All-cause leavers 5,771 to 44,144 a year on roughly 0.9-1.1 million teachers (about 0.6% to 4%); resignations about 0.1%.
4. **Trend verdict.** Mixed and policy-driven: spikes in 2016 (dismissals) and 2023 (early-retirement reform), no underlying upward trend in voluntary resignation, which remains very low.
5. **Definition.** Sector exits from the ministry payroll by all causes; the istifa breakdown isolates voluntary resignation. No official rate is published; rates above are computed against MEB teacher stocks. Not a proper leaving-rate series, but usable multi-year counts.
6. **Additional searches.** "öğretmen istifa oranı MEB istatistikleri"; "öğretmen istifa sayısı MEB istatistik yıllara göre"; ERG Eğitim İzleme coverage (no exit-rate series found in retrieved material).

## Israel: verified; two official multi-year measures

1. **What exists.**
   - **Primary.** CBS (Central Bureau of Statistics), press release 098/2025, *Teaching Staff in the Educational System, 2024/25* (25 March 2025), [PDF](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/mediarelease/DocLib/2025/098/06_25_098b.pdf), read in full: 10,847 teaching staff left the education system in 2021/22 against 10,123 the year before (+7.2%); across 2000/01-2021/22 annual leaving rates (שיעורי העזיבה) were stable at about 5.2% on average, lower in Arab education (3.9%) than Hebrew education (5.6%). The release also documents entries (13,905 joined in 2024/25) and a falling share of new teachers.
   - **Cohort series (our held numbers verified).** First-year leaving rose from 5.8% for the 2010 entry cohort to 10.8% for the 2018 cohort, with five-year cohort attrition of 16.5%; verified in the peer-reviewed policy analysis [Teacher shortage in Israel: a policy analysis of recruitment, preparation, and retention](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02607476.2025.2563153), *Journal of Education for Teaching* (2025), which cites CBS data (secondary confirmation of the CBS cohort figures; Knesset Research and Information Centre compilations report the same pattern, for example one in ten new teachers leaving in the first year, via [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/yokra14873123), secondary).
   - Education at a Glance 2025 places Israel among the lowest attrition countries, under 3% of fully qualified teachers in 2022/23 (1.5% retiring, 0.6% resigning); the gap with the CBS 5.2% reflects a narrower OECD definition (fully qualified teachers, profession exit) against the CBS system-leaving measure.
2. **Span.** 2000/01-2021/22 (annual leaving rates); 2010-2018 entry cohorts (first-year and five-year attrition); 2024/25 stocks.
3. **Level and range.** Overall system-leaving stable around 5% (4-6%); first-year cohort leaving 5.8% to 10.8%.
4. **Trend verdict.** Mixed: overall leaving rate stable (about 5.2% average, no trend), but early-career attrition roughly doubled across the 2010-2018 cohorts.
5. **Definition.** CBS measures exits from the education system workforce (not necessarily the profession forever; returners are tracked separately, 2,281 re-entered in 2024/25). The cohort measure is a proper new-teacher attrition rate. Our held series (5.8% to 10.8%) is confirmed at source level.

## Mexico: NO multi-year evidence found

1. **What exists.** Fragmentary only. The INEE figure for 2018 of 61,376 teachers who retired or deserted (pensionaron o desertaron), reported with a projection of 160,166 for 2023, via [Observatorio del Instituto para el Futuro de la Educación, Tec de Monterrey](https://observatorio.tec.mx/crisis-docencia/) (secondary; single data point, retirement and desertion conflated). Academic literature is qualitative: Cervantes Holguín, "Vocación frustrada: aproximaciones a la deserción docente en México" ([ResearchGate record](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/352927454_Vocacion_frustrada_aproximaciones_a_la_desercion_docente_en_Mexico), abstract-only access). Analysts note that SEP/USICAMM do not publish statistics on retirements, resignations and leaves; vacancy management is decentralised to the states ([Educación Futura](https://www.educacionfutura.org/puebla-entre-la-corrupcion-y-los-procesos-transparentes-de-admision-usicamm/), secondary).
2. **Span.** One year (2018) plus a projection.
3. **Level and range.** 61,376 exits in 2018 on roughly 1.2 million basic-education teachers would be about 5%, but the figure includes pensions and cannot anchor a leaving rate.
4. **Trend verdict.** Not assessable.
5. **Definition.** Mixed retirement-plus-desertion count; not a proper leaving rate.
6. **Searches before concluding absence.** "deserción/abandono docente SEP tasa estadística"; ""abandono de la profesión" OR attrition docentes México estudio bajas maestros renuncias"; ""Vocación frustrada" deserción docente México"; "México bajas docentes SEP por año estadística renuncias jubilaciones USICAMM serie". ## Colombia: NO multi-year evidence found

5. **Definition.** n/a.
6. **Searches before concluding absence.** "deserción docente Colombia retiro maestros oficiales tasa estudio"; ""deserción docente" Colombia magisterio Fundación Compartir OR tasa de retiro OR abandono de la profesión docente"; "teacher turnover attrition Colombia tasa anual salida magisterio Bertoni OR Ome OR Brutti"; IDB publications on teacher scarcity and temporary teachers (Elacqua et al.; the 2020 BID scarcity paper PDF was inaccessible at access date, and search snippets show no Colombian exit-rate series in it).

## Chile: verified and extended; the richest evidence base in this group

1. **What exists.**
   - **Elige Educar and Larraín Vial (October 2022), "Retención, Rotación, Salida de Aula y Reincorporación Docente en Chile (2004-2021)"**, [executive summary PDF](https://eligeeducar.cl/content/uploads/2022/11/resumen-ejecutivo-estudio-retencioxxn-rotacioxxn-salidas-de-aula-y-reincorporacioxxn-docente.pdf), register-based (Mineduc data). This is the study behind our held figure: mean annual classroom-exit rate 9.4% over 2004-2021, ranging from 6.1% (2020) to 15.0% (2010). The same study finds total permanent abandonment (never returning) of 2.96% over 2004-2020, so most annual "exits" are temporary (revolving door).
   - **CIAE Universidad de Chile, Observatorio de Formación Docente, Informe OFD No. 12, "Abandono docente en el sistema educativo chileno 2015-2024"** (published January 2025; researchers Juan Pablo Valenzuela and Francisco Meneses), [full PDF](https://intranet.ciae.cl/uploads/resources/687/2BA%20OFD%2012%20Version%20final+portada_1738090190928.pdf), [CIAE news summary](https://ciae.uchile.cl/noticia/abandono-docente-en-la-educacion-publica-llega-solo-a-4-anual-y-profesores-se-mantendrian-25-anos-en-ejercicio). Read in full. Definition: pedagogy-qualified classroom teachers in the Mineduc Cargos Docentes register who disappear for three consecutive years (returners not counted as leavers). Annual abandonment, all sectors: 2015: 5.5%; 2016: 5.6%; 2017: 4.0%; 2018: 4.5%; 2019: 4.7%; 2020: 3.6%; 2021: 4.9%. Public (municipal/SLEP) sector: 5.9, 6.4, 3.2, 3.7, 3.8, 2.7, 4.5 for 2015-2021; particular subvencionado: 4.8, 4.6, 4.5, 4.9, 5.1, 3.7, 4.8; fully private: 6.4, 7.2, 5.3, 6.6, 6.8, 7.3, 7.6. The sharp public-sector fall from 6.4% (2016) to 3.2% (2017) is attributed to the Ley de Carrera Docente (Ley 20.903, applied from 2016-2018); a 4% rate implies about 25 years average service.
   - **Orrego-Tapia, V. (2024), "Deserción y (re)ingreso a la docencia de aula en Chile entre 2021-2022"**, *Revista de Estudios y Experiencias en Educación* 23(52), 142-160, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.21703/rexe.v23i52.2369): 9.1% desertion between 2021 and 2022 (7.5% left the school system entirely, 1.5% left classroom teaching), against 11.5% re-entry, confirming the revolving-door pattern.
2. **Span.** 2004-2021 (Elige Educar), 2015-2021 with data to 2024 (CIAE), 2021-2022 (Orrego-Tapia). Annual.
3. **Level and range.** Classroom-exit measure: mean 9.4%, range 6.1-15.0%. Stricter three-year-absence abandonment: 3.6-5.6% overall; public sector 2.7-6.4%.
4. **Trend verdict.** Falling then stabilising in the publicly funded sectors: public-sector abandonment roughly halved after 2016 (6.4% to 3.2%) and stabilised around 3-4% (2.7% in 2020, 4.5% in 2021 with pandemic effects); fully private schools rising to 7.6% by 2021; the long classroom-exit series is broadly flat to declining since its 2010 peak. **Verification of holdings:** mean 9.4% for 2004-2021 confirmed (note it is a classroom-exit rate including temporary exits, not permanent profession abandonment); "public sector down to 3-4% post-2018" confirmed for 2017-2020 on the CIAE three-year-absence measure (3.7%, 3.8%, 2.7%), with 4.5% in 2021.
5. **Definition.** Elige Educar: annual exit from classroom teaching (includes temporary exits; permanent abandonment much lower at 2.96%). CIAE: exit from the school system for at least three consecutive years, the closest available proxy for a proper profession-leaving rate.
