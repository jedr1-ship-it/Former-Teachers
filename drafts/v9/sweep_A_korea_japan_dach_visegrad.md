# Sweep A: Korea, Japan, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Czechia, Hungary, Slovak Republic, Poland

Question per country: does any multi-year evidence exist on the evolution of the teacher leaving rate (exits from the teaching profession or nearest equivalent)? All sources accessed 28 August 2026. British spelling throughout.

---

## Korea (Republic of)

**Verdict: YES, multi-year evidence exists and is now deepened into rate terms. Trend: RISING.**

1. **What exists.**
   - Official annual data on retirements and mid-career exits are collected in the KEDI Educational Statistics Yearbook (교육통계연보) and in Ministry of Education (교육부) personnel data, routinely released through parliamentary requests. KEDI statistical service: [https://kess.kedi.re.kr/](https://kess.kedi.re.kr/) (yearbook portal: [https://school.kedi.re.kr/](https://school.kedi.re.kr/)).
   - Ministry of Education data released to lawmaker Kwon Eun-hee (권은희), reported in Segye Ilbo, 25 July 2023: [https://www.segye.com/newsView/20230725514017](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20230725514017).
   - Ministry of Education data on mid-career exits (중도퇴직) 2019-2023 released via lawmaker Baek Seung-ah (백승아) and the Korean Teachers' Union Federation (교사노동조합연맹), reported in Dailian, 30 September 2024: [https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1412254](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1412254).
   - Academic register-based study: 초등교원의 교직 이탈 현황과 관련 요인 탐색: 지역 편차에 따른 쟁점을 중심으로 (Exploring elementary teacher attrition and related factors, focusing on regional disparities), 교육재정경제연구 (Journal of Economics and Finance of Education); analyses provincial office of education data 2009-2018. Abstract only consulted: [https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/articleDetail?nodeId=NODE10704992](https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/articleDetail?nodeId=NODE10704992).

2. **Span.** 2005-2023 across the combined sources (early retirements from 2005; total exits 2017-2022; mid-career exits 2019-2023; academic study 2009-2018).

3. **Level and range.**
   - Total exits (모든 퇴직): 8,367 (school year 2017) to 11,766 (2022), of a teaching workforce of roughly 440,000 at primary and secondary level, i.e. roughly 1.9% to 2.7% a year (rate computed by us, secondary calculation).
   - Honorary (early) retirements (명예퇴직): 3,934 (2017) to 6,525 (2022); 47.0% of all exits in 2017, 55.5% in 2022. Consistent with the series already held (869 in 2005 to 6,594 in 2021, KEDI).
   - Mid-career exits excluding mandatory retirement (중도퇴직): 6,151 (2019), 6,512 (2020), 6,642 (2021), 6,774 (2022), 7,626 (2023, +12.6% year on year, widely attributed to the aftermath of the Seoul Seo2cho teacher suicide). Five-year mid-career exit rates relative to the workforce: elementary 1.50%, middle 2.43%, high school 1.93%; for teachers with under five years of service the ordering reverses (elementary 0.54%, middle 0.30%, high 0.29%).
   - Voluntary resignations proper (의원면직): 640 in 2022, within a wider "voluntary" category (의원면직 including 명예퇴직) of 7,165 in 2022 versus 4,567 in 2017 (+56.9%).
   - Academic study: national attrition rate averaged under 2% a year over 2009-2018, with large regional variance (abstract-level finding).

4. **Trend.** Rising on every component: total exits +40.6% 2017-2022; early retirements +65.9% 2017-2022; mid-career exits +24.0% 2019-2023.

5. **Definition.** Exits from public school teaching posts (profession exit for practical purposes, since public schools dominate); disaggregated by reason (mandatory retirement, honorary early retirement, voluntary resignation). The mid-career series (중도퇴직 = all exits before mandatory retirement age) is a proper leaving-count series; rates as % of workforce are published for 2019-2023 by school level and computable for other years from KEDI workforce denominators. Not school-level turnover.

## Japan

**Verdict: YES, an official triennial series, now extended to FY2024. Total separations FALLING, non-retirement exits RISING.**

1. **What exists.**
   - MEXT School Teacher Statistical Survey (文部科学省 学校教員統計調査), triennial census covering separations (離職) during the preceding fiscal year, by reason including 定年 (mandatory retirement), 病気/うち精神疾患 (illness/mental illness), 転職 (job change), 家庭の事情 (family reasons). Survey portal: [https://www.mext.go.jp/b_menu/toukei/chousa01/kyouin/1268573.htm](https://www.mext.go.jp/b_menu/toukei/chousa01/kyouin/1268573.htm).
   - FY2022 survey, confirmed results (published 27 March 2024), containing the FY2012-FY2021 waves: overview [https://www.mext.go.jp/b_menu/toukei/chousa01/kyouin/kekka/k_detail/1395309_00005.htm](https://www.mext.go.jp/b_menu/toukei/chousa01/kyouin/kekka/k_detail/1395309_00005.htm), summary PDF [https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20240321-mxt_chousa01-000030586_1.pdf](https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20240321-mxt_chousa01-000030586_1.pdf).
   - FY2025 survey, interim report (published 22 July 2026), adding the FY2024 wave: overview [https://www.mext.go.jp/b_menu/toukei/chousa01/kyouin/kekka/k_detail/2025.htm](https://www.mext.go.jp/b_menu/toukei/chousa01/kyouin/kekka/k_detail/2025.htm), summary PDF [https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20260722-mxt_chousa01-000050797_1.pdf](https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20260722-mxt_chousa01-000050797_1.pdf).
   - Full tables on e-Stat: [https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/files?toukei=00400003&tstat=000001016172&page=1](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/files?toukei=00400003&tstat=000001016172&page=1).
   - Secondary confirmation: Nikkei, July 2026, on record 1,319 mental illness exits in FY2024: [https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUD226W80S6A720C2000000/](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUD226W80S6A720C2000000/) (paywalled, abstract-level).

2. **Span.** Exit-year waves FY2012, FY2015, FY2018, FY2021, FY2024 (with a longer archive back to the 1980s on e-Stat).

3. **Level and range (public schools, persons; extracted directly from the two MEXT summary PDFs).**
   - Elementary total separations: 18,020 (FY2012), 17,649 (FY2015), 16,619 (FY2018), 15,030 (FY2021), 13,840 (FY2024). Of which non-retirement (定年以外): 6,015; 6,007; 6,384; 7,024; 8,334 (60.2% of all exits in FY2024 against 33.4% in FY2012).
   - Junior high total: 8,700; 8,280; 9,059; 8,477; 8,371. Non-retirement: 3,744; 3,459; 3,671; 3,640; 4,751.
   - High school total: 6,331; 5,340; 5,246; 5,601; 5,325. Non-retirement: about 1,775 (FY2015) rising to 2,455 (FY2024).
   - Mental illness exits (elementary + junior high + high school): 619 (FY2015), 782 (FY2018), 950 (FY2021), 1,319 (FY2024), a record and the first time above 1,000; non-retirement exits across the three levels totalled 15,540 in FY2024, of which roughly 8.5% were for mental illness.
   - As rates (our calculation against Basic School Survey public-school headcounts of roughly 415,000 elementary, 230,000 junior high, 160,000 high school teachers): elementary non-retirement exits rose from about 1.4% (FY2012) to about 2.0% (FY2024); total separation rates fell from about 4.3% to about 3.3% as the retirement wave passed. MEXT publishes counts, not rates; rate conversion is straightforward and stable because denominators are near constant.

4. **Trend.** Mixed by component and MIXED overall: total separations falling (retirement wave receding), non-retirement (voluntary and health-related) exits clearly rising in every school type, mental illness exits more than doubling FY2015-FY2024.

5. **Definition.** Separation from teaching posts in the surveyed school sector during the fiscal year, by reason; excludes transfers within school types but counts moves out of teaching. Close to a profession-exit measure for the public sector; the non-retirement component is the policy-relevant leaving measure. A proper count series with computable rates.

## Germany

**Verdict: YES, now deepened well beyond the retirements-only series: a 15-year exit-rate series as % of the workforce exists. Trend: total exits STABLE-HIGH; the non-age-related share RISING strongly.**

1. **What exists.**
   - Dieter Dohmen (FiBS, Forschungsinstitut für Bildungs- und Sozialökonomie), "Massenexodus der Lehrkräfte? Eine Analyse der Abgänge aus dem Schuldienst", Policy Paper No. 6, 2nd expanded edition, Berlin, 8 December 2024: [https://www.fibs.eu/fileadmin/user_upload/Literatur/Lectures/FiBS_Policy_paper_006_Massenexodus_der_Lehrkraefte_241208_final_2._Auflage.pdf](https://www.fibs.eu/fileadmin/user_upload/Literatur/Lectures/FiBS_Policy_paper_006_Massenexodus_der_Lehrkraefte_241208_final_2._Auflage.pdf). Based on the official school statistics (Destatis/KMK) on Lehrkräfte-Abgänge.
   - Update: Dohmen, "Massenexodus der Lehrkräfte? Eine Analyse der Abgänge aus dem Schuldienst bis einschließlich Schuljahr 2024/25", FiBS Policy Paper No. 9, Berlin, March 2026: [https://table.media/assets/briefings/bildung/fibs_policy_paper_lehrkrafte_ausstieg_2026.pdf](https://table.media/assets/briefings/bildung/fibs_policy_paper_lehrkrafte_ausstieg_2026.pdf).
   - Secondary coverage: News4teachers, February 2025, "Auf zwei Lehrkräfte, die das Pensionsalter erreichen, kommen mittlerweile fünf, die vorzeitig den Schuldienst quittieren": [https://www.news4teachers.de/2025/02/studie-auf-zwei-lehrkraefte-die-das-pensionsalter-erreichen-kommen-mittlerweile-fuenf-die-vorzeitig-den-schuldienst-quittieren/](https://www.news4teachers.de/2025/02/studie-auf-zwei-lehrkraefte-die-das-pensionsalter-erreichen-kommen-mittlerweile-fuenf-die-vorzeitig-den-schuldienst-quittieren/). Statista mirrors the series to 2024: [https://de.statista.com/statistik/daten/studie/1560865/umfrage/anteil-der-ausscheidenden-lehrkraefte](https://de.statista.com/statistik/daten/studie/1560865/umfrage/anteil-der-ausscheidenden-lehrkraefte) (secondary).
   - KMK documentation of Lehrereinstellungsbedarf und -angebot (context on flows): [https://www.kmk.org/fileadmin/Dateien/pdf/Statistik/Dokumentationen/Dok_2_Bericht_LEB_LEA_2024.pdf](https://www.kmk.org/fileadmin/Dateien/pdf/Statistik/Dokumentationen/Dok_2_Bericht_LEB_LEA_2024.pdf).
   - This complements the retirements-only Destatis civil-service pension series 1993-2024 (incapacity share collapse after 2001) already held.

2. **Span.** School years 2010/11 to 2024/25 (gap 2016/17-2017/18 in the underlying reporting; from 2014/15 Bavaria, Lower Saxony and some city states drop out of the comparable panel).

3. **Level and range (general schools, comparable Länder panel; Tables 1-2 of the 2024 paper, verified in full text).**
   - Gross exits as % of the teacher stock: 10.9% (2010/11), 11.3% (2011/12), 9.8%, 9.6%, 9.6%, 9.9%, 10.6% (2018/19), 11.8% (2019/20, peak), 11.2%, 10.4%, 10.6%, 9.5% (2023/24), 9.9% (2024/25). Average 10.4%.
   - Permanent exits from the school system (dauerhafte Abgänge, i.e. excluding moves between schools/Länder, secondments and temporary leavers): average 5.4% of the stock; 5.0% in 2024/25.
   - Composition shift: the age-related share of permanent exits fell from 42-46% (2010/11-2015/16) to 28% (2022/23 and 2023/24); non-age permanent exits ("sonstige Abgänge") rose from about 2.5% of the stock up to 2015/16 to a peak of 4.5%, easing to 3.6% in 2023/24.
   - Länder range 2023/24: 3.0% (Hamburg, Hesse) to 9.4% permanent exits (Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania); 2024/25: just under 3% (Hesse) to 8.6% (Saxony-Anhalt).

4. **Trend.** Gross exit rate broadly STABLE at a high level (9.5-11.8%); the structure has shifted markedly, with early, non-age-related permanent exits RISING (roughly 2.5% to 3.6-4.5% of the stock) and age-related exits falling; roughly five early leavers for every two who reach pension age.

5. **Definition.** Sector exit from the school service of a Land, computed from official school statistics; "dauerhafte Abgänge" approximates profession exit but cannot exclude moves to private schools in some cases or re-entries; explicitly not resignations-only and not retirements-only. It is a proper leaving-rate series (numerator exits, denominator stock), with the caveat of incomplete Länder coverage after 2014/15.

## Austria

**Verdict: NO multi-year leaving-rate series found; only fragmentary counts and one-off statements. Trend: NOT ESTABLISHABLE from published data.**

1. **What exists (fragments only).**
   - Rechnungshof (Court of Audit), "Lehrpersonaleinsatz", report 2025/18: [https://www.rechnungshof.gv.at/rh/home/home/2025_18_Lehrpersonaleinsatz.pdf](https://www.rechnungshof.gv.at/rh/home/home/2025_18_Lehrpersonaleinsatz.pdf). Contains a retirement prognosis (Pensionierungsprognose, Abbildung 1) and notes that among teachers who resigned in 2022/23, more than half (primary) to almost two thirds (secondary) had under five years of service; no exit-rate time series.
   - Vienna Bildungsdirektion figures via ORF Wien: 192 amicable contract terminations among over 10,000 Vienna teachers in 2021/22 ([https://wien.orf.at/stories/3138174/](https://wien.orf.at/stories/3138174/)); 151 dissolutions, resignations or retirements August 2022 to end January 2023 ([https://wien.orf.at/stories/3194547/](https://wien.orf.at/stories/3194547/)). Union and authority disputed the scale.
   - Parliamentary statement (FPÖ enquiry debate, 4 December 2025): the education minister reported that fewer than 5% of career-change entrants (Quereinsteiger) leave teaching again: [https://www.parlament.gv.at/aktuelles/pk/jahr_2025/pk1142](https://www.parlament.gv.at/aktuelles/pk/jahr_2025/pk1142). Profil on early Quereinsteiger exits: [https://www.profil.at/oesterreich/lehrermangel-die-ersten-quereinsteiger-schmeissen-hin/402765415](https://www.profil.at/oesterreich/lehrermangel-die-ersten-quereinsteiger-schmeissen-hin/402765415).

2-4. **Span, level, trend.** No published series of exits as counts or rates spanning multiple years was found; the fragments above cover single school years or sub-populations and cannot support a trend verdict.

5. **Definition issues.** The Vienna figures mix resignations, amicable terminations and retirements (sector exit from one Land's service); the RH material is retirements-prognosis plus a one-year tenure breakdown of resignations.

6. **Searched before concluding absence.** Statistik Austria "Lehrpersonen" pages (headcounts only, exits explicitly not offered: [https://www.statistik.at/statistiken/bevoelkerung-und-soziales/bildung/lehrpersonen](https://www.statistik.at/statistiken/bevoelkerung-und-soziales/bildung/lehrpersonen)); BMBWF parliamentary Anfragebeantwortungen (e.g. 14791/AB XXVII. GP, which concerns qualification recording, not exits: [https://www.parlament.gv.at/dokument/XXVII/AB/14791/imfname_1578949.pdf](https://www.parlament.gv.at/dokument/XXVII/AB/14791/imfname_1578949.pdf)); Rechnungshof 2025/18 full text (grep for Kündigung, Austritt, Abgänge); the dropout-lehramt.at research network; German-language searches: "Lehrer Berufsausstieg Österreich Statistik", "Dienstaustritte Kündigungen Lehrpersonen jährlich", "Lehrer:innen Drop-out Studie Längsschnitt". Nothing resembling a national multi-year leaving-rate series surfaced.

## Switzerland

**Verdict: YES, register-based longitudinal evidence in two federal waves plus cantonal monitoring, but no continuous annual national rate series. Trend: broadly STABLE (exit levels similar across the 2010s; retention high).**

1. **What exists.**
   - BFS (Federal Statistical Office), "Mobilität der Lehrkräfte der obligatorischen Schule", BFS Aktuell, Neuchâtel, March 2014 (author contact Jacques Babel), based on the school staff statistics (SSP) register: [https://www.bfs.admin.ch/bfsstatic/dam/assets/348836/master](https://www.bfs.admin.ch/bfsstatic/dam/assets/348836/master).
   - BFS, "Verbleib der Lehrkräfte an der obligatorischen Schule" (Prospektive Statistiken/Verbleibsanalysen), October 2022, longitudinal register linkage 2015-2020: [https://www.swissstats.bfs.admin.ch/data/webviewer/appId/ch.admin.bfs.swissstat/article/issue221522192200-01/package](https://www.swissstats.bfs.admin.ch/data/webviewer/appId/ch.admin.bfs.swissstat/article/issue221522192200-01/package). Media synthesis: SRF, "Lehrpersonen bleiben ihrem Job überraschend treu": [https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/neue-studie-zum-lehrermangel-lehrpersonen-bleiben-ihrem-job-ueberraschend-treu](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/neue-studie-zum-lehrermangel-lehrpersonen-bleiben-ihrem-job-ueberraschend-treu) (secondary).
   - Canton Zurich Bildungsdirektion, "Beschäftigungssituation der Lehrpersonen in der Volksschule" (ongoing cantonal monitoring): [https://www.zh.ch/de/bildung/bildungssystem/zahlen-fakten/beschaeftigungssituation-der-lehrpersonen-in-der-volksschule.html](https://www.zh.ch/de/bildung/bildungssystem/zahlen-fakten/beschaeftigungssituation-der-lehrpersonen-in-der-volksschule.html).

2. **Span.** 2010-2011 (2014 study), 2015-2020 (2022 study), 2016/17-2023/24 (Zurich monitoring).

3. **Level and range.**
   - 2014 BFS study: one-year exit rate from the compulsory-school teaching workforce 2010 to 2011: 8.4% of persons (7.0% weighted by lessons); 16% in the first year of service, under 5% beyond 10 years of service; roughly 49% of new teachers projected to leave within their career on those rates; about 23% of leavers re-entered within a year (temporary exits are large).
   - 2022 BFS study: of teachers active in 2015, 80% were still employed at a school five years later (90% of the under-55s; 41% of the 55+); 61% of younger leavers returned within four years; about 2.5% of all teachers (55+) left permanently between 2015 and 2016.
   - Zurich: one-year exit rate from the cantonal school system 7%; 79% of all teachers (and 69% of newly hired) still in the Zurich system after seven years (2016/17-2023/24).

4. **Trend.** STABLE to slightly improving retention; the 2022 federal study and the Zurich series were both read publicly as showing teachers "surprisingly loyal", against expectations formed by the 2014 mobility study. Strict comparability between the two BFS waves is limited (different metrics), so a formal national trend line cannot be drawn.

5. **Definition.** Exit from employment at any school in the register (system exit, not profession exit in the strict sense; permanent versus temporary exits are separated in both BFS studies). Rates are proper leaving rates for the years measured, but published as two study waves rather than an annual series.

6. **Also searched.** BFS Lehrpersonen/Szenarien pages ([https://www.bfs.admin.ch/bfs/de/home/statistiken/bildung-wissenschaft/szenarien-bildungssystem/obligatorische-schule-lehrpersonen.html](https://www.bfs.admin.ch/bfs/de/home/statistiken/bildung-wissenschaft/szenarien-bildungssystem/obligatorische-schule-lehrpersonen.html)), LCH press archive ("Den Schulen laufen die Lehrer davon", NZZ am Sonntag 2014: [https://www.lch.ch/fileadmin/files/documents/LCH_in_den_Medien/Lehrerberuf/140406_Den_Schulen_laufen_die_Lehrer_davon_NZZ_am_Sonntag.pdf](https://www.lch.ch/fileadmin/files/documents/LCH_in_den_Medien/Lehrerberuf/140406_Den_Schulen_laufen_die_Lehrer_davon_NZZ_am_Sonntag.pdf)), search terms "BFS Lehrpersonen Berufsausstieg Verbleibsquote", "Berufsverläufe Lehrkräfte Austritt".

## Czechia

**Verdict: NO official leaving-rate series; one strong retrospective academic study. Trend: NOT ESTABLISHABLE as an annual series.**

1. **What exists.**
   - Miroslava Federičová and Filip Pertold, "Odchody z učitelské profese v Evropě" (Exits from the teaching profession in Europe), IDEA at CERGE-EI, study 6/2022, March 2022: [https://www.avcr.cz/export/sites/avcr.cz/.content/galerie-souboru/tiskove-zpravy/2022/IDEA_Studie_6_2022_Odchody_z_ucitelske_profese_0324.pdf](https://www.avcr.cz/export/sites/avcr.cz/.content/galerie-souboru/tiskove-zpravy/2022/IDEA_Studie_6_2022_Odchody_z_ucitelske_profese_0324.pdf). Based on SHARE retrospective career histories (cohorts born up to ~1966, careers spanning the 1970s-2010s): 31% of Czech teachers left the profession at some point in their working life (Europe: south 15%, Nordics 37%); 25% of profession leavers go within the first two years and 35% within ten; each year of experience lowers the exit probability by about 2 percentage points; 59% of central European leavers moved to other high-skilled jobs. Lifetime shares, not an annual rate series.
   - MŠMT, "Mimořádné šetření ke stavu zajištění výuky učiteli, srovnání 2019 a 2025" (extraordinary teacher-supply survey): [https://edu.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Mimoradne-setreni-ucitele-Srovnani-vybranych-zakladnich-vystupu-v-letech-2019-a-2025.pdf](https://edu.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Mimoradne-setreni-ucitele-Srovnani-vybranych-zakladnich-vystupu-v-letech-2019-a-2025.pdf). Covers shortages, ageing (teachers 63+ up 70% between 2019 and 2025) and unqualified-teacher share (6.6% to 10.4%), but not exits.
   - Masaryk University (Komenský journal), on beginning teachers: explicitly states that beginning-teacher drop-out "is not captured by state-guaranteed statistics" in Czechia: [https://old.ped.muni.cz/komensky/clanky/zacinajici-ucitele-a-odchody-ze-skolyskolstvi-vyznam-profesniho-akterstvi](https://old.ped.muni.cz/komensky/clanky/zacinajici-ucitele-a-odchody-ze-skolyskolstvi-vyznam-profesniho-akterstvi).
   - Intention data (not exits): SYRI/PAQ Research surveys, e.g. 13% of teachers considering leaving; PAQ Research education briefs: [https://www.paqresearch.cz/post/v-navrhovanem-rozpoctu-msmt-chybi-pres-50-miliard-ukazujeme-14-oblasti-kde-usetrit-a-kam-investovat/](https://www.paqresearch.cz/post/v-navrhovanem-rozpoctu-msmt-chybi-pres-50-miliard-ukazujeme-14-oblasti-kde-usetrit-a-kam-investovat/).

2-4. **Span, level, trend.** The IDEA study gives lifetime exit incidence and timing (retrospective, up to 2017 interview waves) but no year-by-year national leaving rate; no MŠMT or ČŠI annual exit series was found. Trend over time cannot be established from published Czech sources.

5. **Definition.** IDEA: profession exit at any point in the career (proper profession-exit concept, but cumulative lifetime measure, survey-recall based). Ministry surveys measure shortage and stock structure, not exits.

6. **Searched before concluding absence.** MŠMT analyses pages ([https://msmt.gov.cz/vzdelavani/skolstvi-v-cr/skolskareforma/analyzy-a-vyzkumy](https://msmt.gov.cz/vzdelavani/skolstvi-v-cr/skolskareforma/analyzy-a-vyzkumy)) and statistical yearbook environment; ČŠI thematic reports; IDEA/CERGE-EI catalogue; PAQ Research; EDUin audits; Czech terms "odchody učitelů ze školství", "odchodovost učitelů", "fluktuace učitelů data MŠMT", "podíl odcházejících učitelů ročně". No annual exit counts or rates located.

## Hungary

**Verdict: PARTIAL. No official continuous rate series, but register-based academic evidence (2002-2008) plus state-school exit counts for 2021-2023 that show a RISING trend.**

1. **What exists.**
   - Varga Júlia, "A közalkalmazotti béremelés hatása a tanárok pályaelhagyási döntéseire" (The effect of the public sector wage increase on teachers' decisions to leave the profession), Budapest Working Papers on the Labour Market BWP 2013/4, MTA KRTK KTI: [https://www.econstor.eu/bitstream/10419/108496/1/bwp1304.pdf](https://www.econstor.eu/bitstream/10419/108496/1/bwp1304.pdf). Register-based (50% population admin panel, 2002-2008, 57,546 teachers, 51-52% of the profession); Cox and competing-risk hazard models of profession exit; earnings opportunities significantly affect exit; the 2002 wage rise temporarily reduced exit probability. Multi-year, register-based, profession-exit definition; hazards rather than a published annual rate series.
   - Tankerület (state school district) data via átlátszó.hu, 1 December 2023: 3,738 teachers left state schools in 2021, 5,316 in 2022 (+40%); 4,314 resignations in state schools in Jan-Nov 2023, about 5% of state-school teachers: [https://atlatszo.hu/oktatas/2023/12/01/a-pedagogusok-ot-szazaleka-osszesen-4314-tanar-mondott-fel-iden-az-allami-iskolakban/](https://atlatszo.hu/oktatas/2023/12/01/a-pedagogusok-ot-szazaleka-osszesen-4314-tanar-mondott-fel-iden-az-allami-iskolakban/). Corroborating: Index, 29 July 2023 (about 5,000 left in a year): [https://index.hu/belfold/2023/07/29/oktatas-statusztorveny-tanar-pedagogus-felmondas/](https://index.hu/belfold/2023/07/29/oktatas-statusztorveny-tanar-pedagogus-felmondas/); government counter-figure of 1,205 exits after the status law (Rétvári, October 2023): [https://index.hu/belfold/2023/10/05/pedagogusok-tanarok-statusztorveny-oktatas-felmondas/](https://index.hu/belfold/2023/10/05/pedagogusok-tanarok-statusztorveny-oktatas-felmondas/).
   - Early-career risk study: "Veszélyben a legfeljebb 5 és 10 éve tanító pedagógusok? A pályaelhagyást előrejelző lehetséges tünetek", Iskolakultúra 33(10), 2023: [https://www.iskolakultura.hu/index.php/iskolakultura/article/view/44757](https://www.iskolakultura.hu/index.php/iskolakultura/article/view/44757) (predictors, not rates).
   - Older subject-pattern study: "A tanári pályaelhagyás szaktárgyi mintázata", Educatio 2012/4: [http://epa.oszk.hu/01500/01551/00062/pdf/EPA01551_educatio_2012_04_607_618.pdf](http://epa.oszk.hu/01500/01551/00062/pdf/EPA01551_educatio_2012_04_607_618.pdf).

2. **Span.** 2002-2008 (register panel); 2021-2023 (state-school counts); KRTK indicator volumes cover entry (2-3% a year) but, on inspection of the 2019 edition, contain no teacher profession-exit indicator ([https://kti.krtk.hu/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/A_kozoktatas_indikatorrendszere_2019.pdf](https://kti.krtk.hu/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/A_kozoktatas_indikatorrendszere_2019.pdf)).

3. **Level and range.** State-school leavers: 3,738 (2021) to 5,316 (2022); resignations 4,314 (2023), roughly 5% of the state-school teaching workforce (about 100,000+; KSH counts about 140,000-149,000 teachers in all sectors).

4. **Trend.** RISING sharply 2021-2023 around the 2022-2023 pay disputes and the status law; longer-run rate evolution not published.

5. **Definition.** The 2021-2023 figures are sector exits/resignations from state-maintained (tankerületi) schools only, excluding church and foundation schools, and do not verify whether leavers quit teaching altogether; Varga (2013) uses a proper profession-exit definition but for 2002-2008. No proper continuous leaving-rate series exists.

6. **Also searched.** KSH oktatás pages and Oktatási adatok bulletins ([https://www.ksh.hu/oktatas](https://www.ksh.hu/oktatas), headcounts only); Oktatási Hivatal/KIR; Hungarian terms "pedagógus pályaelhagyás statisztika", "pályaelhagyó pedagógusok aránya", "hány pedagógus hagyta el a pályát".

## Slovak Republic

**Verdict: NO multi-year leaving-rate series; the ministry's own analysts state the fluctuation time series is too short. Trend: NOT ESTABLISHABLE.**

1. **What exists (fragments only).**
   - IVP (Inštitút vzdelávacej politiky, Ministry of Education), Balberčáková and Miklošovič, "Prognóza počtu žiakov a učiteľov podľa okresov do roku 2030", Komentár 1/2023, March 2023: [https://www.minedu.sk/data/att/c51/25814.a49b30.pdf](https://www.minedu.sk/data/att/c51/25814.a49b30.pdf). Models retirements only and states explicitly (footnote) that non-retirement exits are not modelled because a longer time series on staff fluctuation is not yet available ("keď budeme mať k dispozícii dlhší časový rad údajov o fluktuácii zamestnancov").
   - IVP cross-sectional exit gradient, reported via SME: exit rates from the education sector fall from about 20% for beginning teachers to about 3% for those with 18+ years of practice; projected shortage 1,500-2,100 teachers a year: [https://www.sme.sk/domov/c/slovensku-moze-v-nasledujucich-rokoch-chybat-1-500-az-viac-nez-dvetisic-ucitelov-rocne](https://www.sme.sk/domov/c/slovensku-moze-v-nasledujucich-rokoch-chybat-1-500-az-viac-nez-dvetisic-ucitelov-rocne) (secondary; underlying IVP analysis).
   - IVP, "Atraktivita učiteľského povolania" (2019/2020 survey analysis; graduate placement: only 55% of 2014-2017 teaching graduates employed in education by 2016/17-2017/18): [https://analyzy.gov.sk/files/archiv/14/ATRAKTIVITA-UCITELSKEHO-POVOLANIA.pdf](https://analyzy.gov.sk/files/archiv/14/ATRAKTIVITA-UCITELSKEHO-POVOLANIA.pdf).
   - To Dá Rozum (MESA10), Jozef Miškolci, "Odliv učiteľov" analysis, 2019 (survey and TALIS-based, intentions and shortage, no exit rates): [https://analyza.todarozum.sk/docs/329245002ai0a/](https://analyza.todarozum.sk/docs/329245002ai0a/).
   - NÚCEM/IVP research on fluctuation factors (2018-2019, PIAAC Online, about 900 staff; job-search intentions, no exit rates): [https://www.minedu.sk/vyskum-ucitelov-ake-faktory-vplyvaju-na-fluktuaciu-ucitelov/](https://www.minedu.sk/vyskum-ucitelov-ake-faktory-vplyvaju-na-fluktuaciu-ucitelov/).
   - IVP, "Už mi, lásko, není dvacet let: starnutie učiteľskej populácie", Komentár 4/2023, November 2023 (stock structure 2018-2022, retirement-age share 4.2-4.4%): [https://www.minedu.sk/data/att/dcb/28258.ddf690.pdf](https://www.minedu.sk/data/att/dcb/28258.ddf690.pdf).

2-4. **Span, level, trend.** Only a cross-sectional exit-by-experience gradient (about 20% first-year down to 3% at 18+ years) and stock/ageing series; no published year-by-year exit counts or rates, hence no trend verdict.

5. **Definition.** The IVP gradient concerns exits from the education sector (sector exit); the prognosis models retirements only.

6. **Searched before concluding absence.** CVTI SR statistical yearbooks (headcounts; graduate placement data used by IVP); minedu/IVP komentáre a analýzy; analyzy.gov.sk archive; To Dá Rozum; Slovak terms "učitelia odchod z profesie", "odchodovosť učiteľov zo školstva", "fluktuácia učiteľov", "miera odchodov". No multi-year rate series located.

## Poland

**Verdict: NO leaving-rate series; the ministry itself states SIO cannot identify profession exits. Fragmentary counts suggest RISING exits around 2022-2023 but no proper series exists.**

1. **What exists (fragments only).**
   - Ministry (MEN) statements: on SIO data to end-2023, about 4,500 planned departures in the second half of 2023 (1,961 retirements, 2,233+ contract terminations, 383 to the compensation benefit), plus 14,375 teachers starting health leave in 2022/23; Infor.pl, 9 August 2023: [https://samorzad.infor.pl/sektor/edukacja/nauczyciele/6231451,ministerstwo-edukacji-informuje-o-liczbie-nauczycieli-odchodzacych-z-pracy-do-konca-2023-r.html](https://samorzad.infor.pl/sektor/edukacja/nauczyciele/6231451,ministerstwo-edukacji-informuje-o-liczbie-nauczycieli-odchodzacych-z-pracy-do-konca-2023-r.html).
   - MEN, answering a 2025/2026 parliamentary question, states that on SIO data it is not possible to establish precisely how many teachers left the profession in 2023-2026, because SIO records contract terminations but cannot follow individuals' further careers (reported by Strefa Edukacji: [https://strefaedukacji.pl/44-proc-wiecej-i-cztery-dekady-pracy-men-ma-recepte-na-kryzys-kadrowy/ar/c5p2-28123759](https://strefaedukacji.pl/44-proc-wiecej-i-cztery-dekady-pracy-men-ma-recepte-na-kryzys-kadrowy/ar/c5p2-28123759), secondary).
   - Prawo.pl on the 2023 staffing crisis (young-age cohorts shrinking in SIO; vacancy counts 11,000-13,000 on kuratoria boards 2021-2022): [https://www.prawo.pl/oswiata/braki-kadrowe-w-szkolach-2023,522893.html](https://www.prawo.pl/oswiata/braki-kadrowe-w-szkolach-2023,522893.html) (secondary).
   - Academic/local study: "Młodzi nauczyciele odchodzą ze szkoły. Raport z diagnozy uwarunkowań odchodzenia z zawodu przez warszawskich nauczycieli o stażu pracy do 5 lat" (Young teachers leave school: determinants of profession exit among Warsaw teachers with up to 5 years of service), University of Warsaw Press, 2024: [https://wuw.pl/data/include/cms//Mlodzi_nauczyciele.pdf](https://wuw.pl/data/include/cms//Mlodzi_nauczyciele.pdf). Qualitative/survey diagnosis, not a rate series.
   - Intention surveys: IBE and union surveys report roughly one third of teachers under 30 not planning to stay beyond five years, and about 12% of education staff wanting to leave (secondary summaries, e.g. [https://strefaedukacji.pl/odchodza-ze-szkol-i-zaczynaja-od-nowa-takie-zawody-wybieraja-nauczyciele/ar/c5p2-29102619](https://strefaedukacji.pl/odchodza-ze-szkol-i-zaczynaja-od-nowa-takie-zawody-wybieraja-nauczyciele/ar/c5p2-29102619)).

2-4. **Span, level, trend.** No multi-year exit counts or rates published; single-period counts (2012 reduction of 6,400 teachers after planned redundancies; about 4,500 planned departures H2 2023) are not comparable over time. Media and union material point to rising exits around the 2022-2023 pay disputes, but this cannot be quantified as a rate trend from published sources.

5. **Definition.** Available figures mix retirements, compensation-benefit take-up and contract terminations (employer exit, not profession exit); MEN explicitly concedes profession exit is unmeasurable in SIO as published.

6. **Searched before concluding absence.** GUS (stat.gov.pl) education releases "Edukacja w roku szkolnym..." (teacher headcounts only: [https://stat.gov.pl/files/gfx/portalinformacyjny/pl/defaultaktualnosci/5488/21/1/1/edukacja_w_roku_szkolnym_2022-2023-wyniki_wstepne.pdf](https://stat.gov.pl/files/gfx/portalinformacyjny/pl/defaultaktualnosci/5488/21/1/1/edukacja_w_roku_szkolnym_2022-2023-wyniki_wstepne.pdf)); SIO documentation (pomoc.sio.gov.pl); Sejm interpelacje archive (orka2.sejm.gov.pl, 2012-2013 answers located); IBE project pages; Evidence Institute; ZNP material; demagog.org.pl factcheck (403, inaccessible); Polish terms "nauczyciele odejścia z zawodu", "liczba nauczycieli którzy odeszli SIO", "wskaźnik odejść nauczycieli".
