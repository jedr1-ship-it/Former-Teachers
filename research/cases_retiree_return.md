All facts below carry a source that was actually opened or seen in search results; items that could not be verified are flagged [UNVERIFIED].

## 1. Austria — Reactivation of retired teachers (Länder-level recruitment)

### 1.1 Identification

- **Official name:** None. This is not a single programme but a set of ad hoc measures by Austria's nine regional education directorates (Bildungsdirektionen), taken amid the baby-boomer retirement wave; there is no dedicated legal instrument (APA/ORF, 2023). The federal ministry's umbrella teacher-recruitment strategy "Klasse Job" (launched autumn 2022) is the closest national frame, but the retiree outreach itself is Länder-driven (Rechnungshof, 2025).
- **Responsible bodies:** Bildungsdirektionen of the Länder (esp. Vienna, Styria, Salzburg, Vorarlberg); Federal Ministry of Education (BMBWF) for federal-school staff.
- **Timing:** Active recruitment documented from December 2022 (Vienna letters) and reported nationally in March 2023 (APA/ORF, 2023); re-employment of pensioners recorded by the Court of Audit from school year 2018/19 onwards (Rechnungshof, 2025).

### 1.2 Verified facts (reconstruction of the truncated Excel cell)

- **Vienna:** In December 2022 the Vienna Bildungsdirektion wrote to 300 already-retired teachers ("300 bereits pensionierte Pädagoginnen und Pädagogen per Brief") and instructed school principals to speak with staff nearing retirement about staying on (APA/ORF, 2023; also carried by vienna.at and Tiroler Tageszeitung, 11 March 2023).
- **Styria:** School leaders were encouraged to ask retirement-age teachers about continuing, including at reduced hours; some retirees accepted (APA/ORF, 2023).
- **Salzburg:** Contacted all retired teachers up to age 67; offered public-transport job tickets and higher premiums; flagged that crediting prior service periods (Vordienstzeiten) from fixed-term contracts was a barrier requiring federal legislative change "already underway" (APA/ORF, 2023).
- **Vorarlberg:** Created a dedicated service point, "Arbeitsplatz Schule", about a year before March 2023, to inform principals and teachers about options for working into/in retirement; re-entry information is sent out together with retirement paperwork (APA/ORF, 2023).
- **Take-up (snapshot, March 2023):** Vorarlberg 42 retired teachers in service (including those who never fully stopped), Upper Austria 36, Tyrol 10; at compulsory schools 42 persons nationwide in 2022/23, plus roughly 30 at federal schools according to the ministry (APA/ORF, 2023; TT, 2023 — exact German wording: "An den Pflichtschulen ... sind es im laufenden Schuljahr über alle Bundesländer hinweg 42 Personen, an den Bundesschulen sind es laut Bildungsministerium etwa 30"). Note the internal tension in the APA figures (Land-level counts exceed the "nationwide" compulsory-school count; definitions evidently differ — e.g. Vorarlberg's 42 includes "Weiterbeschäftigte", i.e. teachers who stayed past retirement age, not only returners).
- **Cumulative take-up (audited):** Between school years 2018/19 and 2023/24, **1,062 retired teachers in total resumed teaching across Austria** (Upper Austria 227, Tyrol 49), with the highest intakes in 2021/22 and 2022/23; the Court of Audit notes "this trend did not continue" and stresses the temporary character of the measure (Rechnungshof, 2025, pp. 24-25 of report "Lehrpersonaleinsatz", Reihe Bund 2025/18). Context: Austria employs roughly 123,000 teachers (APA/ORF, 2023).
- **Pay/pension arrangements:** Returning retirees are hired on new (typically fixed-term or special) contracts alongside their pension; the Salzburg directorate stated that prior-service crediting for such contracts required federal legislative amendment (APA/ORF, 2023). The Excel's specific claim that returnees "keep their full pension and take a separate contract paid at the beginner salary scale" is consistent with the non-crediting problem but **[UNVERIFIED]** in openly accessible sources — it most plausibly derives from Resch (2025), which is paywalled (confirmed no open-access copy via Unpaywall). Recommend keeping only if someone with library access confirms it against Resch (2025).
- **2025 study:** Resch (2025), European Journal of Teacher Education (advance online, 10 July 2025), asks in its subtitle exactly the Excel's question ("An untapped reserve or emergency response?"). Method: qualitative interviews with retired teachers and experts (n = 14; nine retired-teacher interviews form the empirical base). Findings: post-retirement career decisions are complex processes shaped by individual, family, school-related and system-level factors; continuing professional roles after retirement and staying in contact with the former school aid the decision to return (Resch, 2025, abstract; Taylor & Francis metadata via Crossref). The Excel's statement that the paper "points to pension and service-crediting rules as a barrier that federal legislation is being asked to fix" matches what the Salzburg Bildungsdirektion said in the APA report (APA/ORF, 2023); whether the paper itself says this is **[UNVERIFIED]** (paywall).

### 1.3 Corrections to the Excel

1. **Truncation fixed** (cell cut off after "reduced hours"): full facts as above — Vienna letters (Dec 2022, 300 retirees + principal outreach), Styria principal outreach, Salzburg all-retirees-to-67 contact + job tickets/premiums, Vorarlberg "Arbeitsplatz Schule" service point.
2. **"Take-up ... on the order of dozens nationwide" is misleading.** Dozens is the March-2023 snapshot; the audited cumulative figure is 1,062 returners over six school years (2018/19-2023/24), concentrated in 2021/22-2022/23 and falling thereafter (Rechnungshof, 2025). Suggest citing both.
3. **"Paid at the beginner salary scale"** — flag as [UNVERIFIED] pending full-text check of Resch (2025).
4. **Source resolution:** "News in ORF" = ORF, oesterreich.ORF.at, 11 March 2023 (APA agency copy also at vienna.at, tt.com, studium.at, Heute.at). "2025 Paper" = Resch (2025), full citation in bibliography. **Add** Rechnungshof (2025) as the strongest (audited) source on scale — not in the Excel at all.

### 1.4 Filter verdict

**KEEP.** Directly on-topic (active re-recruitment of retirees by education authorities), now verifiable with an audited national take-up figure (1,062) plus a peer-reviewed 2025 study — one of the few European cases with both.

## 2. China — National Silver Age Teachers Action Plan (国家银龄教师行动计划)

### 2.1 Identification

- **Predecessor:** Silver Age Lecturing Plan (银龄讲学计划), Implementation Scheme issued by the Ministry of Education and Ministry of Finance in July 2018 (document 教师〔2018〕7号): open recruitment of retired principals, teaching-research staff (教研员), special-grade and senior teachers to teach in rural compulsory-education schools, prioritising deeply impoverished areas ("three regions, three prefectures"); target 10,000 recruits over 2018-2020; minimum service one school year (MOE and MOF, 2018). A parallel plan for universities supporting western regions followed in 2020 (State Council, 2023).
- **Current instrument:** National Silver Age Teachers Action Plan, issued 14 July 2023 (released end-August 2023), document 教师〔2023〕6号, jointly by **ten** bodies: Ministry of Education, Ministry of Science and Technology, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, Ministry of Civil Affairs, Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, National Health Commission, National Healthcare Security Administration, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and China Association for Science and Technology (MOE et al., 2023).
- **Responsible body:** Ministry of Education (Teacher Affairs Department) leads implementation.

### 2.2 Verified facts (reconstruction of the truncated Excel cell)

- **Target:** Build a silver-age teacher corps of roughly **120,000 nationwide within about three years** of 2023 (MOE et al., 2023). Important nuance: the 120,000 covers **five strands** — higher education, vocational education, basic education, lifelong education and private education — and includes full-time, part-time and volunteer service, not only re-employment in rural schools (MOE et al., 2023; State Council, 2023).
- **Eligibility/age:** Basic-education strand: retired principals, teaching-research staff, special-grade and senior teachers, generally **aged 65 or under**; other strands (higher, vocational, lifelong, private education) generally 70 or under, with age limits relaxable for online teaching; participants should generally hold associate-senior or higher professional titles (intermediate acceptable in basic education) (State Council, 2023; MOE et al., 2023).
- **Incentives:** Participants keep their full pension. For the rural/basic-education lecturing strand, working funds are set at **20,000 yuan per person per year**, shared between central and local budgets — fully central in western provinces, 50:50 central-provincial in central provinces, provincially funded in eastern provinces (MOE and MOF, 2018; confirmed for the 2025 round by People's Daily Online, 2025 and R.I.S.E. Magazine, 2025). Host provinces/schools additionally provide housing, once-per-semester round-trip travel, and insurance cover (MOE et al., 2023).
- **Target areas (basic education):** county level — counties recently lifted out of poverty, underdeveloped ethnic-minority counties, old revolutionary base counties, border counties, plus Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps divisions (MOE et al., 2023; People's Daily Online, 2025).
- **Roles:** teaching, demonstration lessons, lesson observation and seminars, serving as vice-principals, and **mentoring young local teachers** (People's Daily Online, 2025; MOE, 2025).
- **Uptake:** By mid-2023, the two predecessor schemes had cumulatively recruited **over 20,000 retired primary/secondary teachers** and nearly 1,000 retired professors from "double first-class" universities (State Council, 2023). A new round under the Action Plan was launched in October 2025 to recruit **7,000 retired teachers in 2025** for county schools (MOE, 2025; People's Daily Online, 2025).
- **Results/evaluations:** No independent impact evaluation located. Chinese commentary and reporting note both positive framing (bridging urban-rural teacher-quality gaps — SCMP, 2025) and a warning that some localities use silver-age recruits to cut formal teaching posts or delay hiring new full-time teachers (reported in R.I.S.E. Magazine, 2025, drawing on Chinese coverage of the 2025 round) [treat as secondary; no official source located].
- **Status 2026:** Ongoing; the 2025 recruitment round was announced October 2025 (MOE, 2025); the three-year 120,000 horizon runs to ~2026. No official tally of progress toward 120,000 located as of August 2026 [not located].

### 2.3 Corrections to the Excel

1. **Truncation/precision:** "run by the Ministry of Education since 2018" — the 2018 scheme was jointly MOE + Ministry of Finance; the 2023 expansion is MOE + nine other bodies (ten total), confirmed.
2. **"120,000 retired teachers over three years"** — correct number, but it is the target size of the overall silver-age corps across five education strands (including volunteers and part-timers), not solely re-employment in rural schools. Recommend rewording.
3. **"20,000-yuan annual allowance, jointly funded by central and local government"** — verified for the rural lecturing strand, but the cost split varies by region (west: fully central; centre: 1:1; east: provincial) (MOE and MOF, 2018).
4. **"generally 65 or under"** — true for the basic-education strand; up to 70 for the other strands (State Council, 2023). Worth a footnote.
5. The clause "places where the money compensates exactly the friction that prevents that return, with little deadweight" is the team's own interpretation, not a sourced fact — keep only as authorial analysis.
6. **Source resolution:** "Government Notice" = MOE et al. (2023) (gov.cn official text; MOE mirror). "2025 Case Study" is ambiguous — the two plausible candidates found are Zhang, Fang and Jiang (2025) (peer-reviewed article covering China's teacher-shortage policies incl. the plan) and the SCMP feature (Wu, 2025). **[UNVERIFIED which one the team meant — ask the Excel's author; both are cited below.]** Recommend citing the primary documents (2018 and 2023 notices) regardless.

### 2.4 Filter verdict

**KEEP.** The world's largest retiree re-employment scheme in education, fully verifiable from primary government documents with explicit targets, allowances and eligibility; weak on independent outcome evidence (note this in the paper), but indispensable for international comparison.

## 3. Portugal — Rehiring of retired teachers under Decree-Law 51/2024

### 3.1 Identification

- **Legal instrument:** Decreto-Lei n.º 51/2024, de 28 de agosto (Diário da República, 1.ª série, n.º 166, 28-08-2024), "exceptional and temporary measures" to staff public pre-school, basic and secondary education; part of the "+Aulas +Sucesso" plan of the Ministry of Education, Science and Innovation (MECI; minister Fernando Alexandre), announced June 2024 (Decreto-Lei 51/2024; SPN, 2024; CNN Portugal, 2024). Procedure regulated by Despacho n.º 10982-A/2024 (SPGL, 2024).
- **Responsible body:** MECI / Direção-Geral da Administração Escolar (DGAE).

### 3.2 Verified facts — and a key correction

The decree contains **two distinct measures the Excel (and much of the press) conflates**:

- **Article 5 — hiring of retired teachers (aposentados/reformados):** fixed-term public-employment contracts to cover temporary needs in shortage recruitment groups or understaffed schools; open only to those retired **five years or less**; requires effective classroom teaching; returnees **keep their full retirement/old-age pension plus an additional compensation pegged to the pay index of the 1st step (1.º escalão) of the teaching career salary scale, pro-rated by the number of teaching hours assigned** (full timetable = 20 weekly teaching hours in pre-school/1st cycle, 14 hours in other levels); annual quota (contingente) set by government despacho; candidates ranked in a DGAE electronic pool by professional graduation at retirement (Decreto-Lei 51/2024, Art. 5).
- **Article 6 — the €750 supplement:** a monthly **acréscimo remuneratório of €750.00** for teachers who already meet the legal conditions for retirement but **stay on** in effective teaching duties, payable from the month after reaching pensionable age, conditional on remaining until the end of the school year (Decreto-Lei 51/2024, Art. 6).

**Correction:** The Excel's "retired teachers return ... while keeping their pension and receiving a monthly supplement of up to 750 euros" attaches the €750 to the wrong group. Per the decree, returnees (Art. 5) get pension + 1st-step-indexed compensation by hours taught; the €750 (Art. 6) is for those postponing retirement. Several outlets (DN, 2024; SÁBADO, 2024) used the "up to €750" shorthand for the returnee scheme, which is where the confusion likely entered.

- **Scale and uptake (2024/25):** Government target: **200 retired teachers** (DN, 2024; Renascença, 2024b). Applications ran 30 September - 11 October 2024 (Renascença, 2024a; Público, 2024 [headline seen in search results; article itself paywalled/403]). **79-80 retirees applied** (79 per Renascença, 2024b; 80 per SÁBADO, 2024); **63 were accepted** ("Regressam 63", DN, 3 December 2024; SÁBADO, 2024); of these, **55 applications were validated** in the first placement wave, with classes starting January 2025; average age ≈ 68; nearly half available for Greater Lisbon/Setúbal Peninsula; most common groups: Portuguese (8), History (6), special education (6); further recruitment waves were planned through the year (Renascença, 2024b). Reported motives of returnees: love of teaching, recovering frozen service time, and insufficient pensions (DN, 2024).
- **Union reactions:** Fenprof secretary-general Mário Nogueira: "Os professores se se aposentam é porque não querem voltar a estar no ativo, a ideia de que os aposentados iriam voltar é uma ilusão" (teachers retire because they do not want to be active again; the idea they would return is an illusion) (SÁBADO, 2024). In a Público/P2 farewell interview (13 April 2025) he laughed off the €750 incentive for himself: "Os 750 euros por mês? ... Não, não faria sentido" (Público/P2, 2025). FNE secretary-general Pedro Barreiros: "É sinal que o desafio não é atrativo. O cansaço acumulado e a escola em que vivemos não é convidativa" (accumulated fatigue; an uninviting school environment) (SÁBADO, 2024); FNE had already lamented the resort to retirees when the plan was announced (CNN Portugal/Lusa, 2024). FENPROF/SPN also criticised the €750 as implausible retention pay relative to pension values (SPN, 2024).
- **Status as of 2026:** The retiree-hiring track continued into 2025/26 (DGAE consolidated pages on contracting of aposentados; SIPE, 2024-25); no published uptake figures for 2025/26 located [not located]. The postponement supplement proved the more used tool: about **2,200 teachers extended their careers** past pensionable age with the €750 in 2025/26 (RTP, 2026; CNN Portugal, 2026). On 30 July 2026 the Council of Ministers restricted the €750 for 2026/27 to shortage areas only — 8 pedagogical-zone frameworks (QZP) covering 235 school units in Greater Lisbon, the Setúbal Peninsula and the Algarve (down from 10 QZP/258 units) — with minister Alexandre saying the blanket version "worked" but "in many cases was not justified" (RTP, 2026; CNN Portugal, 2026).

### 3.3 Corrections to the Excel

1. **€750 misattribution** (see above) — the single most important fix: returnees are paid pension + 1st-step compensation pro-rated by hours; €750/month is the incentive to postpone retirement.
2. **Numbers refined:** ~200 openings (quota), 79-80 applicants, 63 accepted (Dec 2024), 55 validated/placed in the first January-2025 wave. Give dates, since the funnel numbers moved between 3 and 19 December 2024.
3. Eligibility detail worth adding: only those retired ≤ 5 years may return (Art. 5(2)) — relevant to the paper's "re-entry window" discussion.
4. Add 2026 status: continuation in 2025/26, 2,200 users of the postponement supplement, and the July-2026 narrowing to shortage QZPs.
5. **Source resolution:** "Decree-Law 51/2024" = full DR citation below (DGAE PDF verified). "News in Público" = Público (2024) concurso-opening piece, 30 Sep 2024 [paywalled; content verified only via search snippets and Renascença coverage] and/or Público/P2 (2025) Nogueira interview (verified in full via Fenprof's media-archive PDF). "News in Diário de Notícias" = DN (2024), 3 Dec 2024, verified.

### 3.4 Filter verdict

**KEEP.** A rare, fully documented natural experiment in paying retirees to return: legal text, target, applicant funnel, placement numbers, union counter-narrative and a 2026 policy correction are all verifiable — and its low uptake versus the far higher uptake of the stay-on supplement (63 vs 2,200) is itself a policy lesson the paper should highlight.
